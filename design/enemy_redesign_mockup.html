Enemy redesign: every enemy unique

Redesigning all 34 enemies so no two look alike, one role at a time. Each enemy shows its today look next to three concepts, at game scale (1x on a 48 px lane tile) and 3x. Pick one per enemy with the gold buttons; the picks collect at the bottom of the batch for you to copy back. Spec: design/enemy_visuals.md.

  • Every enemy unique: its own silhouette, theme and material; no shared families.
  • Shape first, colour second: turn on Greyscale: each concept must still be recognisable.
  • Contrast with the towers: towers are grey metal with neon accents on hex pads, so enemies carry their identity in coloured bodies and distinct materials (glass, chitin, paper, plasma, lacquer...).
  • Movement unchanged; sizes are free (every radius change is listed for balance).
