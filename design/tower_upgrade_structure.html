Upgrade structure: depth and trunk

Settled: 3 paths per tower, each with its own purpose, and primary/secondary stays. Open: how deep a path goes, and whether the shared trunk (Stock → Retrofit) stays. Pick a trunk and a depth (mix freely) and add notes. Your combination shows the mix on an example tower (today's until you pick). Picks and notes are kept in this browser; copy them from the bottom of the page and send them back. Spec: design/towers.md (issue #55).

  • Mixing (kept): climb up to two paths, up to two upgrades each. The 3rd upgrade on either makes it the primary, which goes on to its mastery; the other is the secondary, capped at 2 and keeping its full effects. The third path is locked.
  • Masteries still need the tower's Mastery research (research is reworked separately, later).
  • The example uses placeholder path purposes (Power, Speed, Effect). Your roster sets the real ones per tower.
  • Prices are set per tower later. The "average step" figure only shows how today's budget would spread over more or fewer steps.
