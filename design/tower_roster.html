  • Weapon classes replace the roles (your call, 2026-10-09): every tower has exactly one, and the class groups the shop. What else it does is TBD.
  • Each tower has 3 paths of 4 upgrades and a mastery, each path with a purpose decided per tower (your examples: Power, Range, Speed, Effect, Buff), plus an optional Retrofit designed for the tower.
  • Primary/secondary stays: climb two paths until one gets its 3rd upgrade; that one is the primary, the other is capped.
  • Counters to the reworked enemies come only through path upgrades; no tower exists just to counter.
